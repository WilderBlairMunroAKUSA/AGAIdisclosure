Exploration:Exploitation::Divergent:Convergent::Associative:**Analytical**

In the context of cognition:  
- **Exploration** aligns with generating new ideas or seeking novel possibilities (divergent thinking).  
- **Exploitation** focuses on refining or applying known ideas effectively (convergent thinking).  
- **Associative** refers to linking disparate ideas through creativity or intuition, akin to exploration/divergence.  
- **Analytical** involves structured reasoning, logic, and narrowing down possibilities, akin to exploitation/convergence.
