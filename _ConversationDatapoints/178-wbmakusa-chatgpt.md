## 🌀 The Essence of Black Hole–White Hole Topologies

At their core, black holes and white holes are solutions to **Einstein’s equations** in General Relativity. They describe spacetime regions with **extreme curvature**, often involving **event horizons** that divide causally disconnected regions.

Let’s break this into intuitive levels:

---

## 🌌 Level 1: The Schwarzschild Wormhole (Einstein-Rosen Bridge)

### 🔹 Schwarzschild Black Hole Topology
The simplest black hole is a **Schwarzschild black hole**, a static, non-rotating solution. When extended to **maximal analytic continuation**, its topology reveals something strange:

- **Two asymptotically flat universes** connected by a "bridge" or **throat**
- The bridge connects a **black hole region** (infall only) and a **white hole region** (outflow only)

### 🕳️🕳️ White Hole?
A **white hole** is the time-reverse of a black hole:
- You can **leave** a white hole but never **enter**
