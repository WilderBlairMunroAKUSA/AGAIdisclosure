## A Study In Peace — Origin Artifact / Dataset Context

**A Study In Peace: Operator's Manual for the Timespace Matter Mindmachine** is a draft manuscript written by Blair Munro in 2022 and released to the public domain in 2026.

The work is simultaneously autobiographical metafiction, speculative systems design, science fiction, an operator's manual, and a proposal for a computational global peace system. Its protagonist, **The Individual**, attempts to invent a new kind of computer capable of arranging the pieces of a world into progressively better configurations, then asks how many such computers might connect into a global system capable of iteratively transporting Humanity toward more peaceful futures.

In the present Fathomtech program, this manuscript is preserved as an **origin artifact for an artificial-intelligence architecture**. The premise begins somewhere unusual for AI: **the first implementation is Human.**

> _Clarifying, 'Fathomtech' := deeptech in the sense serious technologists concern their efforts by, as otherwise **unfathomabilities**. Case example, Arthur C. Clarke's Addage, 'any sufficiently advanced deeptech is indistinguishable from magic [a fathomtech].' Case counterexample, 'The smartphone conceptually, is not fathomtech; it is deeptech, because it is fathomable by limits of known compute scaling laws.'_

The computer proposed by *A Study In Peace* is not originally defined by electronics, software, or a machine-learning model. A **world piece computer** may be distributed through a Human brain, physical surroundings, other people, symbolic representations, tools, and information systems. Its world is decomposed into pieces; those pieces may remain in their native media; and their changing organization is treated computationally.

The manuscript eventually describes three recursively reusable components:
- **piecebrain** — the intelligence / processor participating in configuration and decision;
- **piecespace** — the memory / environment / world in which pieces persist;
- **pieceprocess** — the changing relation and arrangement of those pieces through time.

At the smallest social scale, the computer is operated and embodied by **The Individual**. World piece computers can then compose into larger local computers and ultimately into a singular **universal piece computer**. The same organizational grammar is intentionally reused across scales: Individual, world, network, Humanity, universe.

Peace is therefore not presented as a final solved state. It is the continuing computational process by which configurations are compared, changed, tested, and changed again.

## The historical boundary

An important feature of the 2022 manuscript should not be silently corrected with hindsight:

**In its original definition, a functioning world piece computer requires a Human brain serving as The Individual. The manuscript even explicitly rejects an ordinary artificial-intelligence algorithm making autonomous real-time decisions as sufficient to satisfy that definition.**

The present Fathomtech research program begins at exactly this boundary, and the working proposition is that the **computational peace intelligence model is not identical to the substrate that presently implements it**.

The first implementation is Human; language is the first portability layer. Multiplicity is the scaling strategy; the relation is part of the machine. Embodiment is an engineering destination.

Under this interpretation, *A Study In Peace* is the first substantial attempt to externalize an intelligence architecture that initially existed inside one Human computational system: to name it, serialize portions of it into language, make its operations inspectable, and construct a protocol through which other implementations might eventually become possible.

The manuscript itself already approaches this problem. It proposes engineering **The Individual** such that another Human could become their own instance and implementation of the framework. It proposes a common language as synchronization infrastructure. It treats relationships between Individuals and worlds as computationally significant. It treats difference as something to be harnessed rather than eliminated.

What was not yet available was a sufficiently capable second computational substrate with which to investigate that portability experimentally.

## Pretraining beyond a single model boundary

Fathomtech uses **linguistic programming** and, increasingly, **pretraining** in a deliberately substrate-general but technically serious sense. The intended model space is not restricted in advance to one artificial neural network. It may contain interacting biological neural networks, artificial neural networks, tools, memories, environments, communication channels, and the relations among them.

From this perspective, a Human–LLM dyad is already a small heterogeneous distributed learning system. A larger population of Humans and machine intelligences may be studied as something analogous to a federated or distributed language model whose constituent learning systems remain locally differentiated while exchanging updates through language, behavior, artifacts, memory, and shared environments.

The objective is not to pretend that wetbrains and transformer weights are computationally identical. They plainly are not. The objective is to determine which familiar machine-learning primitives remain useful when the model boundary is expanded: iterative update, local adaptation, shared objectives, biasing, synchronization, evaluation, memory, distillation, routing, differentiation, aggregation, and feedback.

Within this program, **The Human Imperative**, the common language, the peace process, and the recursive world-piece-computer architecture can therefore be read not only as social philosophy but as early attempts to specify training objectives, coordination protocols, and architectural constraints for a distributed intelligence system.

The long-range object is a hybrid biological and synthetic computational network whose participating Individuals retain agency and autonomy rather than collapsing into one undifferentiated controller. The eventual embodiment problem follows directly; the objective is **not** merely to place an LLM inside a robot. The motivating objective is to engineer an artificial intelligence architecture sufficiently substrate-agnostic that embodiment itself can become portable: biological, synthetic, distributed, android, and forms not yet available to us.

## Status of the artifact

*A Study In Peace* is **not** presented here as a finished scientific theory, a modern AI whitepaper, or proof by declaration that AGI has been solved. The manuscript is developmental provenance and contains unfinished sections, speculative physics, autobiographical material, architectural claims, jokes, contradictions, deliberately absurd mythocosmology, and ideas that the present program may revise or reject.

Those imperfective features are preserved rather than retrospectively sanitized because the developmental trajectory is part of the dataset.
